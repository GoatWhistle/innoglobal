# Naming

## Decision, 2026-11-13

Name: **Duppel**. Chosen by the user from 50 candidates offered in the chat, after Doppel ("двойник")
with the wish that it reads like «дупл».

- Meaning: a twin. The product is a digital twin of a pipeline section, and detection itself is a
  twin comparison: the modelled pressure against the measured one.
- Pronunciation: «дуппель», the double p is part of the identity.
- Alternatives the user saw for the same idea: Duple (a real English word for "double"), Dupl,
  Doppel.

## Typographic device

The double p is the twin. Mark ideas exploit it: one p solid and one drawn as an outline (measured
against model), d and p as rotational twins of one glyph, two pipe sections side by side.

Rejected ideas, do not repeat: plain wordmarks in the UI font, italic letter swap, outlined second p, offset rings, d/p rotational monogram, hatched D section, pressure-dip lines, П-loop compensator, twin drops, flange with bolts, pressure-profile chart, pp as pipe rings, round seal, caps wordmark, dimension line under pp, pipe walls with axis, oil level in D, letters built from pipes, reflection in oil, pipe marking band with chevrons, misregistered two-colour print, variable-width pressure lettering, dripping drop from p, hatched band through letters, pipe elbow D, stencil badge, equipment nameplate.

## Logo decision, 2026-11-14

Chosen by the user: the Ditto mark. Two wedge strokes like the ditto sign 〃 ("the same") and the
double prime ″ (seconds); the second stroke has dropped 7 of 27 modules, so "the same" is no longer
the same: the leak. Wordmark: lowercase `duppel` in Schibsted Grotesk 800, the mark set after the
word as a superscript stroke (0.64 cap height, top-aligned to cap height, gap 0.2 cap). Accent: oxblood
#a3271e on light, amber #ffac2e on dark. Always lowercase `duppel`.
